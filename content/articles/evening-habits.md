# Evening Habits — stop wasting after-work hours

## 1. Replace phone time with engaging activities
- **App blocking**: use tools like Opal to schedule blackout periods for distracting apps.
- **Prep alternatives**: keep a list of go-to hobbies (journaling, reading, sewing, puzzles, Substack articles).
- **Digital detox**: consider HubSpot’s 14-day challenge to reset screen habits.

## 2. Time-block evenings intentionally
- **Plan ahead**: allocate slots for hobbies, cooking, reading, rest.
- **Week-level view**: balance activities across the week to avoid overloading one day.
- **Keep flexibility**: schedule downtime without letting it crowd out fulfilling activities.

## 3. Disengage from work mode
- **End-of-day ritual**: shut down laptop, change clothes, or physically leave the workspace.
- **Dual personas**: distinguish “work self” vs “life self” to shift headspace.
- **Physical separation**: avoid the office area after hours or create dedicated zones.

## 4. Start small and evaluate habits
- **Audit routines**: note what you do right after work; pick one habit to adjust.
- **Incremental change**: modify one element at a time to avoid overwhelm.
- **Change scenery**: stop at a café or park before home to mentally switch contexts.

## 5. Use accountability and lists
- **Accountability buddy**: share goals, check in, or plan joint activities.
- **Enjoyment list**: keep a menu of non-work activities to defeat default scrolling.
- **Break familiarity**: use the list to interrupt autopilot routines.
