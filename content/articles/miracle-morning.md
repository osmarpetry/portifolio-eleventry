# Miracle Morning — notes & actions

## ✨ Core idea
- Hitting snooze says “I dislike waking up so much I’ll do it twice.”
- Your last thoughts at night shape how you wake up—plan a mission that excites you.
- Pre-program the morning like launch day; automation (IoT lights, heat, curtains) removes friction.

## 🌅 Morning routine sequence
1. **Immediate wake** → no snooze.
2. **Bathroom reset** → brush teeth, drink water.
3. **Gear up** → change into workout clothes, move body, then shower.
4. **SAVERS** (Silence, Affirmations, Visualization, Exercise, Reading, Scribing) in the order that fits you.

## 🛠 Practical strategies
- Outline tomorrow the night before to wake with purpose.
- Use automation and environment cues to eliminate negotiation.
- Morning meditation + early rising (maybe 5 am, or your “early”) jump-start the day.
- Habits take ~21+ days; after the friction phase you enter pride/identity stage.

## 🔗 Reinforcements
- Revisit limiting beliefs and rewrite supportive ones.
- See also: *Pense e Enriqueça*, *O Poder do Hábito* for habit stacking and belief shifts.
