## 🧾 Elevator pitch (140 characters)
- Senior Front-End Engineer (+6 yrs) shipping performant UX with React/TypeScript/GraphQL/Next.js & Node. Excels at performance, resilient systems, mentoring.

## 🧊 Layoff storyline (X-Team)
- "I was part of a company-wide restructuring. While there, I shipped high-impact React/TypeScript features, optimized performance, and led TDD/system architecture initiatives across AWS/GCP. Leadership trusted me to mentor juniors and unblock teams. I’m ready to bring that momentum to a new team."

## 🧍 Interview flow — Eduardo’s notes (refined)
1. Drink water, slow down breathing.
2. Script tune-up:
   - Experience: “I’ve worked as a software engineer with a front-end focus for 5+ years.”
   - Tooling: “Front-end: React, TypeScript, GraphQL. Back-end: Node.js, AWS/GCP, PostgreSQL. Comfortable going fullstack when needed.”
   - Learning: “Currently deepening back-end/Golang skills to strengthen front/back integration.”
3. Revisit biggest challenges (e.g., RoR) and learning path before interviews.
4. Optional topics: salary band US$35–45/h, PTO, schedule flexibility, notice period.

### Questions for interviewers
- Company values, team size, cross-team interaction, project duration.
- Monitoring tooling and async communication practices.
- Career path, differentiators.
- Reconfirm values: “Which company values resonate most with you?”

## 🧩 X-Team guidance (curated)
- Build a cohesive career narrative: explain short gigs as purposeful moves, avoid mentioning dual employment.
- Use STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioural questions.
- Highlight technical depth: state management, Redux, XState, memoisation, generics, testing strategies.
- For live coding/assessments: rubber-duck with interviewer, clarify pitfalls before coding, divide & conquer, state complexity and trade-offs.
- For FullStack Labs: revisit prior submissions, prepare alternative solutions, keep communication proactive.

## 📝 Reminders & prompts
- Open with context to keep the listener hooked (“Let me frame the scenario before diving into the solution”).
- Fine-tune salary pitch; confirm early bird/night owl preference, timezone overlap, notice period.
- Mention Golang learning and Node.js timeline (first touch 6 yrs ago, 2 yrs professional).
- During timed interviews, focus on simplifying, citing Big-O, and narrating trade-offs when time ends.

## 🔄 Follow-up queue
- Return to FullStack Labs process; analyse past exercises for improvement.
- Continue Golang studies to reinforce the narrative and versatility.

# How to Sell Yourself — Interview Playbook

## 🌐 Links & resources
- Prompting practice: [https://learnprompting.org/docs/introduction](https://learnprompting.org/docs/introduction)
- Books to revisit: *The Software Engineer’s Guidebook*, *The Tech Resume Inside Out*
- Coding practice hubs: BackToBackSWE, Blind 75, LeetCode, AlgoExpert, BigFrontendDev
- Job boards & communities: Strider, WeWorkRemotely, Remotive, Lever jobs (e.g., Strapi), Glassdoor remote filters, Landing.jobs, Otter, Flatworld, Prediktive, Toggl, FloHiring, Braintrust, Refer.me

## 🗣 Interview narrative
- Begin answers with context before the main point to engage listeners.
- Elevator pitch template:
  > "I’m a Senior Front-End Engineer with 6+ years leading React and TypeScript projects end-to-end. I’ve also delivered Node.js services and cloud infrastructure on AWS/GCP. I focus on performance, testing, and cross-team collaboration."
- Communicate calm and confidence: sip water, steady tone, acknowledge learning path and current goals (e.g., expanding backend expertise).

## 💼 Compensation & logistics
- Salary range frame: $35–45/hour (negotiable); invite benefits/PTO discussion.
- Ask about work hours, time zones, and notice period expectations early.

## ❓ Questions for recruiters/clients
- Company values and culture? Team size and structure?
- Interaction model with partner teams? Project timeline (short/medium/long)?
- Monitoring or analytics tooling? Asynchronous communication process?
- Career progression opportunities? Competitive differentiators?

## 🛠 STAR method refresher
1. **Situation** — Set the scene briefly.
2. **Task** — Your responsibility and goal.
3. **Action** — Step-by-step actions, including collaboration details.
4. **Result** — Quantify impact; share lessons and transparency points.

## 🤕 Challenge & layoff messaging
- Focus on impact and learning, not blame:
  > "I was part of a company-wide layoff. During my tenure I shipped high-impact React/TypeScript features, optimized performance, and mentored teammates. I’m now looking to bring that experience to a new team."

## 🧠 Behavioural prep
- Catalogue technical deep dives: state management, Redux, XState, memoization, TypeScript generics, testing strategies.
- Anticipate tough questions; respond with honesty, growth mindset, and STAR structure.

## 🧪 Live coding & assessments
- Use rubber-ducking: restate the problem, highlight edge cases, plan before coding.
- Decompose solutions; explain complexity trade-offs (Big-O) under time pressure.
- For timed assessments, discuss alternative approaches if time expires.

## 🗺 Career story
- Connect short projects or overlaps to a bigger purpose rather than listing gigs.
- Highlight Golang learning or other ongoing upskilling to show adaptability.
