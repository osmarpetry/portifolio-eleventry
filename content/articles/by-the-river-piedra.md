# By the River Piedra — break the glass, break the curse

## ⚡ Core reminder
- “Break the glass!” — shatter the curse the same way you have already broken bigger things and felt relief afterwards.
- If the choice concerns your own happiness, stop prioritising how others might judge it.

## 🎬 Source of inspiration
- Dialogue from *Horsemen*: the rat character finds peace only after letting go of everything, reinforcing the mantra above.

## 💬 Reflection prompts
- What “glass” am I holding onto today that keeps the curse alive?
- Which past moments prove that breaking it leads to freedom, not regret?
- Am I clinging to expectations that belong to other people rather than my own joy?
