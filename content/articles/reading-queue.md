# Reading Queue

## 💼 Interview & problem solving
- **Cracking the Coding Interview** — practice data structures, algorithms, and system design questions.
- **Programming Pearls** — cultivate algorithmic thinking and problem decomposition.
- **Daily Coding Problem** — maintain a steady cadence of coding exercises.
- **Programming Interviews Exposed** — alternative perspectives on interview prep.

## 🧠 Career & communication
- **Never Split the Difference** — negotiation tactics for salary and stakeholder alignment.
- **How to Win Friends and Influence People** — timeless guidance on rapport and influence.
- **The Complete Software Developer's Career Guide** — roadmap for career progression and skill planning.
- **The Imposter's Handbook** — reinforce CS fundamentals and confidence.
