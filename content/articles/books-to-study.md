# Books & Papers to Study

## 🧪 Scientific reading — Requirements Engineering
- **Naming the Pain in Requirements Engineering** — global survey on RE challenges; highlights process pain points.
- **Integrated Requirements Engineering: A Tutorial** (Somerville, 2005) — still a concise guide to elicitation techniques.
- **Status Quo in Requirements Engineering: A Theory and a Global Family of Surveys** — follow-up research expanding on NtPiRE findings.

## 📚 Books that help with requirements
- **Software Engineering** (Sommerville) — canonical chapter on requirements fundamentals.
- **Shape Up** (37signals) — pragmatic approach to shaping and prioritising work; available free online.

## 🧠 Extra theory on code quality
### Research papers
- **Software Aging** — how long-lived systems degrade without proactive care.
- **Design Erosion: Problems and Causes** — practical illustration of architectural decay.
- **An Empirical Study of Design Degradation** — longitudinal look at how projects worsen over time.
- **Dynamics of Software Maintenance** — effort models for keeping software healthy.
- **Why We Refactor? Confessions of GitHub Contributors** — motivations behind refactoring in the wild.
- **Are Two Heads Better than One? On the Effectiveness of Pair Programming** — data-driven assessment of pairing.
- **Assessing Practitioner Beliefs about Software Engineering (2020)** — challenges assumptions around quality vs productivity.
- **What Improves Developer Productivity at Google? Code Quality** — connects quality practices to measurable productivity.
- **Toward a Definition of Cognitive-Driven Development (CDD)** — introduces CDD benefits for refactoring.
- **To What Extent Cognitive-Driven Development Improves Code Readability?** — one-year experiment validating CDD gains.

### Complementary books on quality & maintenance
- **Effective Software Testing** (Maurício Aniche) — testing practices backed by research.
- **Building Maintainable Software** — practical guidance on simplicity, coupling, modularity.
- **Tidy First?** — short, actionable tips for cleaner code.
- **Simple Object-Oriented Design** (Aniche) — pragmatic OO design for maintainability.
- **Práticas de Código para Devs Eficientes** — ebook summarising 20 daily design practices.
- **Philosophy of Software Design** — thought-provoking perspective on complexity and modularity.
- **Good Code Bad Code** — direct comparison of good vs bad coding habits.
