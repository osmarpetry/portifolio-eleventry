# The Software Engineer’s Guidebook
## TL;DR
- Own your career: deliver impact, align goals with business, document wins.
- Pragmatic tech leads balance scope, stakeholders, team health, and delivery.
- Growth to Staff+ needs business insight, cross-team influence, and long-term thinking.
- Continuous learning, adaptability, and healthy teams sustain longevity.
- Interview prep: map stakeholders, tell impact stories, follow up for feedback.
