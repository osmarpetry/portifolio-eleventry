# The Power of Habit — key connections

## 🧠 Personal note
Read during a sleepless night; the ideas on habit loops resonated with my attempts to reset routine and energy.

## 📚 Habit loop (Duhigg)
- Cue → Routine → Reward model explains personal and organisational behaviour.
- Changing habits requires identifying the cue and swapping the routine while keeping the reward.
- Keystone habits cascade into broader improvements (e.g., exercise, morning planning).

## 🏢 Nelson & Winter link
- Firms rely on routines—akin to organisational habits—that evolve via feedback.
- Routines are “genes” defining how companies respond to markets.
- Both theories stress iterative learning and adaptation driven by repeated behaviour.

## 🔄 Shared takeaways
- Behavioural loops govern outcomes at individual and company levels.
- Sustained change needs experimentation, feedback, and replacement behaviours.
- Mapping cues/routines helps restructure workflows and corporate processes.
