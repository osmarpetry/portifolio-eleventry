# Industrial-Inspired Home Design Notes

## 🎯 Design intent
- Create sightlines with large mirrors so every room offers a quick visual check before leaving the house.
- Use an industrial palette (black frames, concrete textures, warm wood) because it pairs easily with future décor experiments.
- Prioritise every decision around daily convenience: quick cleaning, ergonomic reach, and flexible storage.

## 🚿 Bathroom
- **Glass shower box** replaces curtains to eliminate mildew and speed up cleaning.
- **Dual shower heads + handheld sprayer** add comfort and accessibility for varied daily routines.
- Minimalist fixtures keep lines clean and support the industrial aesthetic (matte black or brushed steel).

## 🍳 Kitchen & pantry
- **Custom cabinetry** sized for both residents: shelves within reach for me and for Uli, preventing strain.
- **Tower unit** centralises oven, microwave, and heavy pans; reduces bending and keeps counters free.
- Plan for labelled storage bins to maintain the minimalist look while supporting quick cooking sessions.

## 🚗 Garage & workspace
- Direct access from the garage ensures safe entry, faster grocery unload, and a weather buffer.
- **Tool zone** separated from living areas to keep dust away; doubles as a hobby corner when needed.
- Consider epoxy flooring for durability and water resistance.

## 🪞 Mirrors & lighting
- Oversized mirrors positioned at traffic points reflect natural light and help with outfit checks.
- Combine mirrors with adjustable track lighting to reinforce the industrial tone and highlight textures.
